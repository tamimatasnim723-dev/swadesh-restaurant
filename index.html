Our story

A table set with heritage

Swadesh — meaning "one's own land" — opened its doors in Sylhet with a simple aim: to cook the way Bengali grandmothers cook, without shortcuts. Our kitchen sources hilsa and rui from local rivers, grinds its own spice blends each morning, and lets mustard, panch phoron and slow heat do the work that no shortcut ever could.

The dining room pairs dark timber and brass with soft, low light, so a quick lunch and a three-hour family feast feel equally at home. Whether you're marking a wedding, closing a business deal, or simply craving your mother's cooking, Swadesh is built to hold the occasion.
